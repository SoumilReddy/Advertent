AdvertentBot

User agent: AdvertentBot/1.0

What it does

AdvertentBot reads public product information from Indian apparel brands' websites for Advertent, a discovery app that helps people find homegrown brands. Every product shown in Advertent links back to the brand's own website, where the purchase happens. Advertent never sells products.

What it collects

  • Product facts: name, price, sizes, stock, category, product page URL and image URLs.
  • Public brand information and links to return and shipping policies.

It does not store product descriptions or image files, and it never accesses login, cart or checkout pages.

How it behaves

  • Respects robots.txt, including Crawl-delay.
  • Makes at most one request every 2 seconds per website.
  • Stops on any block, CAPTCHA or access-denied response. It does not use proxies to get around blocks.

How to opt out

Add this to your site's robots.txt:

User-agent: AdvertentBot
Disallow: /

Changes are picked up on the next visit.

Contact and removal requests

To ask for your brand's content to be removed or corrected, email advertentshop@gmail.com. We acknowledge requests within 24 hours and act within 72 hours.
